
Inverse Problems
################

Inverse problems play a central role in scientific computing and engineering, where internal system parameters or governing causes must be determined from indirect, observable effects. While forward modeling predicts physical measurements from known model properties, inverse modeling deduces unknown parameters, initial states, or boundary configurations from experimental or field observations. In SepalSolver, we provide robust numerical methods and optimization routines to formulate and solve these challenging problems./// 
Inverse problems arise across numerous domains, including subsurface characterization, non-destructive testing, medical imaging, and parameter identification in complex dynamical systems. Because these problems are inherently ill-posed—often suffering from non-uniqueness and extreme sensitivity to measurement noise—effective regularization and stable numerical schemes are essential to recover reliable, physically meaningful solutions./// 
In this chapter, we will explore the mathematical foundations of inverse problems, techniques for ill-posedness and regularization, and their practical implementations in SepalSolver through hands-on engineering examples.///
