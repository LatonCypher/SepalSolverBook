
Inverse Problems
################

This chapter covers inverse problems, which involve determining the cause from observed effects.
In this chapter, we will explore various techniques for solving inverse problems, including regularization methods and optimization algorithms.
We will also discuss the challenges associated with inverse problems, such as ill-posedness and sensitivity to noise.
By the end of this chapter, you should have a solid understanding of how to approach and solve inverse problems in practice.
